Moderator Operating Standard

Risk ≠ guilt. Use signals to prioritize review, inspect evidence, document the decision, and escalate credible threats.
1. Preserve the report/evidence.
2. Review related reports, blocks, verification and interactions.
3. Contact no reporter or subject outside approved workflows.
4. Apply the least restrictive safety action that addresses the risk.
5. Record the reason and resolution in the audit trail.
