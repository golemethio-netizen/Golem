1. Who we are

Wana Dating is part of WanaGebya (wanagebya.com), operated by [operator name / business registration]. Contact: [contact email].

2. What we collect

Account details: email address or phone number, password (stored by our authentication provider, not readable by us), your full name and address or location, and an optional phone number. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your Google account email and basic profile from Google.

Profile details: first name, age, city, gender, who you are looking for, goal, lifestyle, family plans, religion, optional occupation and income range, your bio, and up to three photos. Religion and income range are sensitive; they are optional where the form allows it ("Prefer not to say").

Verification: if you request the verified badge, a selfie that is stored privately and seen only by you and moderators.

Activity: likes, matches, profile visits, posts and comments, messages, voice notes, date plans, reports and blocks, call records, notifications, and last-active time.

Payments: if you request Platinum, the transaction number and receipt image you submit.

3. How we use it

To run the service: show your profile to other members, suggest compatible matches, deliver messages and calls, verify profiles, confirm Platinum payments, send notifications you enable, prevent fraud and abuse, and respond to reports.

4. Who can see what

Your public profile (name, age, city, photos, bio and the profile fields above) is visible to other members. Posts can be set to public or matches-only. You can choose who may call you and who sees your last-seen time in Privacy Settings. Blocked members cannot see your profile or message you. Your selfie, payment receipt and account contact details are not shown to other members.

5. Voice calls

Voice calls are set up through our service and then carried between the two members' devices. We keep a record that a call took place; we do not record call audio.

6. Service providers

We use Supabase for our database, sign-in and file storage, Netlify (or our hosting provider) to serve the website, and Google for Google sign-in and web fonts. These providers process data on our behalf, and may store it outside Ethiopia. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Your choices

You can edit your profile and photos, change privacy settings, block members, and change your password in the app. To access, correct or delete your data, contact [contact email].

9. Security

We use access controls and private storage for sensitive files, but no online service is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password and do not share verification codes.

10. Children

Wana Dating is only for people 18 and older. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
